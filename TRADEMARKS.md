# Name and attribution policy

"**ABHED**" is the name of this project, created by **Rajdeep Das** (IEM-UEM, Kolkata, India).

The Apache License 2.0 gives you wide rights to use, change and share the code. It does
**not** give you the right to use the project's name or the creator's name for your own
version (Apache License 2.0, Section 6). This page says what is and is not allowed.

## Allowed without asking

- Redistributing the unmodified project under the name ABHED, with LICENSE and NOTICE.
- Saying truthfully that your work "is based on ABHED by Rajdeep Das" or "uses ABHED".
- Citing ABHED in papers, talks and reports (see CITATION.cff).
- Naming ABHED when you report measurements made with the unmodified project.

## Needs written permission from the maintainer

- Distributing a **modified** version under the name "ABHED" or a confusingly similar name.
  Name your fork something else and keep the NOTICE file, which credits the original.
- Presenting a fork, product or service as the official ABHED, or as endorsed by the creator
  or by the supporting institutions.
- Using the name in a domain name, package name or organisation name.

## Official source

The only official repository is https://github.com/drajdeep/ABHED-v2-native. Official
releases are the tagged releases published there by the maintainer.
