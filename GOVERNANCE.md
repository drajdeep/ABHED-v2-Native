# Governance

ABHED is open source, but it is maintained by a single owner.

- **Maintainer:** Rajdeep Das (GitHub `@drajdeep`), creator and copyright holder.
- The maintainer decides what is merged, what is released, the roadmap, the code points and
  wire formats used, and the licence.
- Only the maintainer can merge into `main` or publish a release. Every pull request needs the
  maintainer's approval (`.github/CODEOWNERS`).
- Contributions are accepted under the [Contributor Licence Agreement](CLA.md), so the project
  stays under one owner who can relicense it.
- The maintainer may appoint co-maintainers. Appointments are recorded in this file and can be
  withdrawn by the maintainer.
- Disagreements are discussed in issues. The maintainer's decision is final.

Forks are welcome under the Apache License 2.0, subject to the name policy in
[TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md).
